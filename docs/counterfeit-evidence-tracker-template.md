# GameDayGrabs Counterfeit Evidence Tracker Template

Private working template for organizing product authenticity evidence before requesting Google Merchant Center Counterfeit policy review.

Do not commit receipts, invoices, order confirmations, private document contents, phone numbers, addresses, payment details, or supplier account details to GitHub. Keep evidence files in a private owner-controlled folder outside the public repository.

## Owner Instructions

- Create one private folder per product using the product ID/SKU below.
- Save owner-taken front product photos, back/sealed packaging photos, and close-up UPC/barcode photos.
- Save receipts, invoices, order confirmations, distributor records, or wholesale proof when available.
- Do not upload receipts publicly.
- Do not paste private receipt, invoice, account, or supplier details into GitHub.
- If proof is missing for a product, consider pausing that product before appeal.
- Do not click "I fixed the issue" until the evidence set is ready.

## Appeal Readiness Checklist

- [ ] 12/12 products have owner-taken front photos.
- [ ] 12/12 products have owner-taken back/sealed packaging photos.
- [ ] 12/12 products have close-up UPC/barcode photos.
- [ ] UPC/barcode photos match the feed GTINs.
- [ ] Receipts, invoices, or order confirmations are recovered where available.
- [ ] Weak-proof products have been reviewed by the owner.
- [ ] No private evidence documents are stored in GitHub.
